Who I Am

I am Aditya Kumar Mohanani, a Class 9 student at PM SHRI Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya, Burhanpur. I started coding with Python and gradually moved toward machine learning, mathematics, AI systems and broader STEM research.

My aim is to understand the ideas underneath technology — not just use libraries or copy code. I am especially interested in the point where mathematical reasoning, algorithms and real-world engineering meet.

Beyond Coding

My interests also include science and space, sustainable technology, quantum computing, engineering, reading, travelling, cricket, table tennis and collecting coins.

Coin collecting has become a separate window into history, geography, currencies and physical artifacts, with examples from many countries and historical periods.

I want to understand why an AI method works, where it fails, how much compute it needs and whether a simpler or more efficient approach is possible.

A project is more valuable when its claims can be tested, measured and reproduced.
